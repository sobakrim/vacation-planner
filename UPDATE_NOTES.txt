v0.6.1 — email-based auto-membership, no custom invitations

- Removed the call to the invite-group-member Edge Function.
- Adding a member now only stores name, exact email, role and contract baseline.
- No invitation email is sent.
- When that person creates/signs in to a Supabase account with the exact same email, claim_memberships() links the account automatically and the group appears.
- Existing accounts are linked immediately when the administrator adds an email already known to Supabase Auth.
- No new SQL migration is required if 006_no_approval_member_invites.sql has already been run.
- invite-group-member can be deleted from Supabase Edge Functions; it is no longer used.
